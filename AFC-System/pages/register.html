Register once. Travel across the whole network.

A single passenger record links your identity, travel card, wallet balance and trip history across every operator on the network.

  • Your IC or passport number becomes your verified passenger reference.
  • A digital travel card is issued to your account immediately.
  • Concession and monthly passes can be added after registration.
  • Wallet balance is protected by a six-digit travel PIN at the gate.
