    About pending top-ups

    A pending record means the payment gateway has accepted the request but has not confirmed the bank transfer. The wallet is only credited once confirmation arrives, usually within 15 minutes.

    About failed top-ups

    Declined payments never reach the wallet. If your bank shows a debit for a failed record, it is reversed automatically within 3 working days.

    Receipts

    A PDF receipt can be generated for any completed top-up. Receipts show the gateway reference and are accepted for company travel claims.

    Note: receipt download is not wired in this prototype.
