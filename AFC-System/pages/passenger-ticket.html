    Presentation guidance

    1. 1 Hold the NFC card flat against the reader panel on top of the gate.
    2. 2 For mobile QR, increase screen brightness and hold 10 cm from the scanner.
    3. 3 Paper barcodes should be unfolded and presented face-down to the laser reader.
    4. 4 Wait for the green lamp before walking through — the barrier opens for 6 seconds.
